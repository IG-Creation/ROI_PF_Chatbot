# ROI & PF Chatbot

> AI-Powered Chatbot for Analysing Managed Pricing, Return on Investment (ROI), and Profit / Pricing Factor (PF).

## Overview
ROI & PF Chatbot is an intelligent analytical chatbot application designed to analyze, calculate, and optimize:
- **ROI (Return on Investment)**: Investment yield, payback period, net profit margin, dynamic price sensitivity, scenario simulation.
- **PF (Profit Factor / Pricing Factor)**: Managed pricing analysis, gross profit vs gross loss ratios, price elasticity, target markups, margin health scorecards.
